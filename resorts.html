Where Do You Actually Conduct The Team Building?

Here is the honest answer: wherever your team can reach comfortably, with enough open ground to play on and a hall to fall back into if it rains. Below is our working list of resorts and destinations across India — sorted by state, filtered by the city your team flies out of.

We don't sell resorts. We match a brief to a ground.

Woosh Biz is a team building company, not a travel agent — we have no property to fill and no commission to protect. When a company asks us "where do you conduct it", we work backwards from four things before we name a single resort.

🚌

Travel time

Anything over two hours each way eats the programme. For a day outing we stay inside a 90-minute radius; longer drives only make sense with a night's stay.

🌳

Open ground

Most of our activities need flat, unbroken lawn — roughly 1,500 sq ft for every 50 people. A pretty resort with only a pool deck cannot host a Woosh Biz day.

🏛️

Indoor backup

Every venue we clear must have a banquet hall that seats the full group. Rain, heat and dust are not "maybe" in India — they are Tuesday.

💰

Per-head budget

Venue, food and stay usually take 60–75% of an offsite budget. We size the destination to what's left for the actual programme, not the other way around.

  • Best for 30–400 people

What We Check Before We Say Yes To A Venue

A resort that looks perfect in photographs can still be wrong for your programme. This is the list we run through — feel free to use it yourself if you're shortlisting on your own.

1. Usable lawn area

Not total green cover — flat, unbroken ground with no flower beds or parked cars in the middle of it.

2. Hall capacity at theatre style

The number the sales sheet quotes and the number that fits comfortably are rarely the same. We ask for both.

3. Power and AV

Enough points near the activity ground, a working generator, and whether a mic and projector are included or billed extra.

4. Kitchen turnaround

Can they serve the full group inside 45 minutes? A slow buffet has ended more agendas than bad weather.

5. Washrooms near the ground

If they're a five-minute walk from where the group is playing, you lose fifteen minutes every single break.

6. Coach access and parking

Whether a 45-seater can actually reach the porch, or your team is walking the last 400 metres with their bags.

7. Alcohol and music policy

Permit status, bar timings and the local sound cut-off — best confirmed in writing before the DJ is booked.

8. Medical distance

How far the nearest hospital is, and whether the resort has a doctor on call. Non-negotiable for adventure formats.

We'll shortlist venues that suit your brief, share honest pros and cons, and coordinate with the property on activity space, timings and setup. Most clients book the resort directly, which keeps the commercials transparent. If you'd rather we handle the booking end-to-end, we can arrange that too.
We've already picked a venue. Will you still come?
Yes, and it happens often. Send us the venue name and we'll check the ground and hall against your group size, then design the programme around what's actually available there. If something genuinely won't work — say, no wet-weather backup for a 200-person outdoor day in July — we'll tell you before you sign, not after.
What's the minimum group size?
We run programmes from about 15 people upward. Below that, most resorts won't offer group pricing and the activity dynamics change — small teams usually get more from an in-office or half-day format than a full outbound day.
Which venue works for 200+ people?
Shamirpet near Hyderabad, Manesar in NCR, the ECR belt near Chennai, Lonavala near Mumbai and Devanahalli near Bengaluru all have properties that comfortably handle 200–600. Above that, you're looking at convention-grade venues — Shamirpet, Jaipur and Goa are the reliable options.
What happens if it rains?
Every venue we clear has an indoor hall that seats the full group, and every outdoor programme we design has an indoor version of the same arc. We decide on the morning itself, in consultation with your coordinator. Nobody stands around waiting for the sky to make up its mind.
How far in advance should we book?
Four to six weeks for a day outing, six to eight for an overnight, and eight to twelve for an annual offsite in peak season (October to February). Goa, Coorg, Corbett and Rann of Kutch need the longest lead — their season is short and inventory goes early.
Can you run something at a venue not on this list?
Certainly. This list covers the destinations we're asked about most, not the limit of where we travel. We've run programmes at company guest houses, factory grounds, farmhouses and hotel banquet halls. Tell us the location and we'll tell you honestly what will and won't work there.
