Group size

The venue and the stay

The biggest difference between this and a single day. Rooms, meals and the venue's own bill sit on top of ours, and a resort an hour out of the city and one in the hills are not the same number.

Where you are

Before
you book
the retreat.

The things people ask us most often about the two-day format.

How long is a 2-day team building retreat, actually?
Two working days with a night in between, built around your schedule rather than a fixed clock. We agree the start, the breaks and the finish with you on the consultation call, because a group flying in the night before needs a different running order from one driving out on the morning of day one.
What do two days give you that one day does not?
All four game categories instead of two, so nobody sits through an entire offsite in the one format that does not suit them. It also moves the facilitated debrief out of the last twenty minutes of a tiring day and gives it two days of material to work with, which is the part that connects the games back to how the team actually works.
Where do 2-day retreats run?
At a resort or an outdoor venue, because the format needs somewhere the team can also sleep and eat. Every category we run works indoors on a hall floor as well as outdoors on a lawn, so the venue needs open ground and a covered space rather than perfect weather.
Do you arrange the resort, food and stay as well?
Both ways happen. The games and the facilitation are always ours; the venue, rooms, food and transport can sit with us or with you, whichever you prefer. Tell us on the call and the quote reflects it. We also keep a list of resorts we have run programmes at, so you are not picking one blind.
What does a 2-day team building retreat cost?
It is quoted per programme rather than off a price list, because four things move it: how many people are coming, which venue you are staying at, where in India you are, and whether the rooms, food and transport are in our scope or yours. Send us those four answers and we will come back with a number.
How many people can join a 2-day retreat?
Small teams, big teams, and everything in between - all are welcome! Our team building activities are scalable and customizable to accommodate groups of various sizes. Whether you're a startup, a department within a company, or a large organization, we've got the perfect activities for you.
Do I have to be athletic or super competitive to participate?
Team-building with Woosh Biz unlocks the inner child of an adult - hidden potential, enhances collaboration, and boosts productivity, resulting in a more cohesive and successful team. Fitness has nothing to do with it.
What if someone on my team has physical limitations or disabilities?
Inclusivity is incredibly important to us. We can adapt our activities to accommodate individuals with physical limitations or disabilities, ensuring that everyone can participate and contribute to the team building experience. Let us know about any specific requirements, and we'll work together to create an enjoyable and inclusive event.
Can we customize the retreat to align with our specific goals?
Absolutely! We understand that every team has unique objectives and desired outcomes. Our team of experienced facilitators will collaborate with you to tailor the activities, challenges, and debrief sessions to address your specific goals and foster the growth and development you're aiming for.
