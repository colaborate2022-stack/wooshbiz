The space

Designed for growth and creativity.

Every session we run somewhere else gets built here first. The rooms are set up to be rearranged — chairs into a circle, tables pushed back, a simulation laid out on the floor.

Modern training infrastructure

Whiteboards, air conditioning and power at every wall — rooms that hold a full day without anyone fidgeting.

An interactive learning environment

Seating that goes from rows to a circle in a minute, because the second half of a session rarely wants the shape the first half did.

A comfortable workspace

A mezzanine of long-bench workstations above the floor — quiet enough to write in while a session runs downstairs.

Engagement-friendly by design

A chess board and a table tennis table live in the meeting room on purpose. Half the good ideas here arrive mid-rally.

Simulation setups

Floor space that clears completely, so a maze, a build challenge or a full activity rig can be laid out and tested before it travels.

A kitchen and a dining hall

Chai gets made here, not ordered in. Lunch happens at one long table, which does more for a team than most agendas.
