Where you run it,
and what moves the price.

Fourteen years of full days for Ernst & Young, Reliance Industries, Aditya Birla, Accor Hotels, Club Mahindra Resorts, Idemitsu Lube India, Itochu Parekh and Arete Services. No two were quoted the same, and these are the four things that decided it.

Group size

The single biggest lever. It sets how many facilitators travel, how much kit comes with them, and whether the group runs as one or splits into parallel tracks.

The venue

Your own office or lawn costs nothing extra. A resort or an outdoor venue adds their bill to ours — we can work to a venue you have already picked, or point you at ones we have run at before.

Where you are

We run projects pan India. Travel and logistics for the crew and the equipment are part of the quote, so a day in Delhi NCR and the same day in another state are not the same number.

What is in scope

Games and facilitation are always ours. Food, stay and transport can sit with you or with us. Tell us which on the call and the quote reflects it.

Planning the venue side too? We keep a list of resorts we have actually run programmes at, with what each one is good for. If a full day is more than you need, the same games run as a 2–4 hour in-office session; if you want the whole thing under the sky, see outdoor team building and offsites.

Before
you book
the day.

The things people ask us most often about the full-day format.

How long is a 1-day team building programme, actually?
One working day, built around your schedule rather than a fixed clock. We agree the start time, the lunch break and the finish with you on the consultation call, because a group that has travelled two hours to a resort needs a different running order from one walking downstairs to its own cafeteria.
What does a full day include that a half-day session does not?
Two game categories instead of one, so the day can pair a loud physical format with a quieter thinking one and give every kind of person on the team their round. It also leaves real time for the facilitated debrief at the end, which is the part that connects the games back to how the team works.
Does a 1-day programme have to be outdoors?
No. Every category we run works indoors on a hall or cafeteria floor as well as outdoors on a lawn, so a full day can run entirely inside your own building, entirely outdoors at a resort, or move between the two. Nothing in the day depends on the weather unless you want it to.
How many people can join a full-day programme?
Small teams, big teams, and everything in between - all are welcome! Our team building activities are scalable and customizable to accommodate groups of various sizes. Whether you're a startup, a department within a company, or a large organization, we've got the perfect activities for you.
Do you arrange the venue, food and stay as well?
The games and the facilitation are always ours. Venue, food and transport can sit with you or with us, whichever you prefer - tell us on the call and the quote reflects it. We also keep a list of resorts we have run programmes at, so you are not picking one blind.
What does a 1-day team building programme cost?
It is quoted per programme rather than off a price list, because four things move it: how many people are coming, whether you are using your own space or a booked venue, where in India you are, and whether food, stay and transport are in our scope or yours. Send us those four answers and we will come back with a number.
Do I have to be athletic or super competitive to participate?
Team-building with Woosh Biz unlocks the inner child of an adult - hidden potential, enhances collaboration, and boosts productivity, resulting in a more cohesive and successful team. Fitness has nothing to do with it.
What if someone on my team has physical limitations or disabilities?
Inclusivity is incredibly important to us. We can adapt our activities to accommodate individuals with physical limitations or disabilities, ensuring that everyone can participate and contribute to the team building experience. Let us know about any specific requirements, and we'll work together to create an enjoyable and inclusive event.
Can we customize the day to align with our specific goals?
Absolutely! We understand that every team has unique objectives and desired outcomes. Our team of experienced facilitators will collaborate with you to tailor the activities, challenges, and debrief sessions to address your specific goals and foster the growth and development you're aiming for.
