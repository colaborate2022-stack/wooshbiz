Dominic CostaBir

Director, aka TorMentor

Started the business with just Rs 350 — will we ever hear the end of that? A strange combination of risk-taking and resilience (falls, and bounces back to risk again), which is exactly why he's knowledgeable and experienced. Set up HTI India and UAE.

He's more than a Mentor, he's a TorMentor, pushing us out of our comfort zones. He claims to be 18 years old. He's friendly, unless you contest his age.

"Fall 99 times, stand up a 100!"

Abdullah Munajem

Director · Riyadh, KSA

A third-generation businessman from the famed Munajem conglomerate. Ran a couple of the family business units before deciding to take his fun and games seriously.

Set up Woosh in 2018 as a place for families to have fun. Families had fun — but corporate honchos lined up too, and toddlers were being jostled by overgrown kids from corporate, so he quickly set up Woosh Biz. Today Woosh Biz Saudi is growing phenomenally and has expanded its footprint into India.

Jervis Fernandes

Chief Operating Officer

An international and national footballer with the heart of a sprinter (he actually won trophies). Passionate about sports and indoor games — and loses horribly at chess.

Happy and amiable, and always up to tricks. Jervis leverages game strategy, teamwork and a resilient sporting spirit to navigate life. All of which make him the ideal team building facilitator, and the right person to lead Woosh Biz.

As COO he facilitates and executes every Woosh Biz project pan India — Ernst & Young, Club Mahindra Resorts, Itochu Parekh, Arete Services, Idemitsu Lube India, Reliance Industries, Accor Hotels, Aditya Birla and more.

PS: he's actually a great guy. He just hired the wrong copywriter.
